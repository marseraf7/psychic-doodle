Caro x Gomoku is a free, ad-free website and app for playing Caro (Vietnamese five-in-a-row) and Gomoku: play friends or matched opponents online, play against computer characters, analyse your games, solve puzzles and learn openings. Google Sign-In is used only to create and sign in to your player account.
