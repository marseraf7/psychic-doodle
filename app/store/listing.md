## English (en)

### Tên app

Caro x Gomoku

### Mô tả ngắn

Play Caro & Gomoku online or vs AI: 4 rules, tournaments, clubs, game analysis.

### Mô tả đầy đủ

Caro x Gomoku is a five-in-a-row board game for phone and desktop. The app and the web version share one server, so app players and web players can play each other.

4 RULE SETS
• Vietnamese Caro (blocked ends): an open four wins; a five blocked at one end still wins.
• Free-style Gomoku: five or more in a row wins.
• Standard Gomoku: exactly five wins; six or more (overline) does not count.
• International Renju: Black is forbidden from double-threes, double-fours and overlines; forbidden points are marked on the board.
An infinite board: drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

PLAY VS THE COMPUTER
31 bot characters from beginner to master level, each with its own playing style and personality. The final boss, BOAT, is powered by the Rapfi engine — a real challenge for strong players. Beat bots to unlock higher levels.

PLAY ONLINE
• Quick match against similarly rated opponents, public rooms, private rooms with invite code and password.
• Tournaments in many formats: Arena, single elimination, double elimination, round robin, Swiss, and group stage into playoffs.
• Clubs: create a club, invite friends, play internal events.
• Challenge friends to Bo1 / Bo3 / Bo5 matches, Glicko-2 ratings and leaderboards.
• Friends list with online status, direct messages, chat bubbles, invite links via your favourite messenger.

LEARN AND ANALYSE
• Analyse your finished games: the engine points out mistakes, missed wins and the best moves.
• Daily tactics puzzles and lessons from basics to advanced.
• Replay any past game and share it with a link.

MORE
• Fully offline play: vs the computer, two players on one device, puzzles.
• 4 languages: Vietnamese, English, Russian, Chinese. Light and dark themes.
• No ads. Free to play.

### Có gì mới — 1.0.0

First release: online play shared between app and web, tournaments and clubs, 31 bot characters plus the BOAT boss, Rapfi-powered game analysis, puzzles and lessons, 4 languages.

## 中文 (zh)

Caro x Gomoku

### Mô tả ngắn

在线五子棋与人机对弈：4种规则、锦标赛、俱乐部、棋局分析。

### Mô tả đầy đủ

Caro x Gomoku 是一款可在手机和电脑上游玩的五子棋游戏。应用与网页版共用同一服务器，两个平台的玩家可以互相对弈。

4种规则
• 越南Caro（两端封堵）：活四获胜；五连被封堵一端仍算获胜。
• 自由五子棋：五连或更多即获胜。
• 标准五子棋：恰好五连获胜；六连或更多（长连）不算。
• 国际连珠（Renju）：黑棋禁着三三、四四和长连，禁着点在棋盘上明确标出。
无限棋盘：拖动平移，双指缩放。

人机对弈
31位电脑棋手，从新手到大师，每位都有独特的棋风和个性。最终Boss BOAT 采用 Rapfi 引擎驱动，对高手也是真正的挑战。战胜棋手即可解锁更高等级。

在线对弈
• 快速匹配同水平对手、公开房间、带邀请码和密码的私人房间。
• 多种锦标赛赛制：竞技场、单败淘汰、双败淘汰、循环赛、瑞士制、小组赛加淘汰赛。
• 俱乐部：创建俱乐部、邀请好友、举办内部赛事。
• 好友挑战 Bo1 / Bo3 / Bo5 对局，Glicko-2 等级分与排行榜。
• 好友列表与在线状态、私信、聊天气泡、通过常用通讯软件发送邀请链接。

学习与分析
• 复盘已结束对局：引擎指出错误着法、错失的胜机和最佳着法。
• 每日战术题与从基础到进阶的课程。
• 回放任意历史棋局，并通过链接分享。

更多
• 完全离线游玩：人机对弈、双人同机、战术题。
• 4种语言：越南语、英语、俄语、中文。浅色与深色主题。
• 无广告，免费游玩。
